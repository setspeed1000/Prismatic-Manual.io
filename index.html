Core objective

Score 6" foam CHROMaBLOCKS into your alliance's 3 SPIRES and the shared center-field PRISM CORE. Earn colour-pattern bonuses (RAINBOW STATE, PRISM TRIAD). Climb the ASCENT TRUSS in the final 20 seconds.

CADathon format

PRISMATIC is a CADathon: design and document a CAD-complete robot. No physical robot is built. Inspection criteria exist as design targets evaluated by judges for buildability and rules-compliance.

Cycling

When a spire reaches capacity and a new block is scored, the oldest (bottom) block ejects via the CYCLE PORT and returns to the field floor. No limit on total blocks scored. Points already awarded are not lost.

LOW / MID  Cycle port ejects home-side (same operational zone as scoring alliance)

HIGH  Cycle port cross-ejects across the centerline into a 24"×24" CONTESTED CHANNEL on the opposing side. Recovery requires crossing the centerline (legal in teleop).

Congestion penalty

Triggered when 2 or more opposing robots have any bumper portion inside the 60" PRISM CORE ZONE at moment of insert. Scores at the next-lower position tier (position 1 is floor, unaffected). Allied robots do not contribute.

Full face bonus

First time each slot face fills to 4 blocks: +5 pts to the alliance holding 3+ of the 4 blocks. A 2-2 split = CONTESTED, no bonus. One award per face per match. Alliance ceiling: +30 pts. Not multiplied by TRIAD.

PRISM TRIAD multiplier curve

Arm a TRIAD by scoring same colour into 3+ different slot faces. Activate at driver station. Multiplies all base placement scoring for 15 seconds. Max 8 activations per alliance per match (2 per colour, 45-sec same-colour cooldown).

Apex flag rules

3 flags per alliance, colour-coded. Velcro-attached to main rail underside at 96" ±0.5". Releases at 5 lb ±0.5 lb pull. Can be grabbed at any point during endgame — but scores as APEX+FLAG only if robot is at ≥78" when scores are final. Robot that grabs flag but only achieves deep ascent scores 22 pts, not 40. Dislodged teammate flag forfeits that flag's bonus unless re-attached before scores final.

Teamlift

All 3 alliance robots must have lowest point ≥ 24" at scores final. PARK does not count. Mixed tiers qualify (e.g. apex+flag + deep + shallow all satisfy teamlift). Flat bonus — not multiplied by TRIAD.

Awarded when a spire's displayed stack contains ≥1 block of every colour. Awarded to the alliance that scored the completing block.

Chroma sweep

+20 pts one-time bonus when an alliance earns their first rainbow in all three spires (Low + Mid + High) in a single match, awarded at the moment the third distinct spire's first rainbow is completed.

Maximum rainbow + sweep total per alliance: 75 pts (Low 20 + Mid 15 + High 20 + Sweep 20).

Momentum surge

At 1:00 remaining: trailing alliance earns +1 pt per block placement (spire or core). Cap: +15 pts total. Determined once at 1:00 — does not toggle if lead changes. Tied score at 1:00 = no surge. Not multiplied by TRIAD.

Full face bonus

See Prism core section. +5 pts per slot face filled 3+ blocks from one alliance. Alliance ceiling +30 pts. Not multiplied by TRIAD.

PRISM TRIAD multiplier

See Prism core section. Multiplies base placement scoring (spires + core inserts) only. First activation 1.300×, diminishes by 0.025× each use, floor 1.125× at 8th. Max 8 activations per alliance per match.

Key rules
